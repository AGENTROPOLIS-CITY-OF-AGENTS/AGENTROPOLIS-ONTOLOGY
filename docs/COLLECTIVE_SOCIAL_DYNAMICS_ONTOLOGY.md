# Collective Social Dynamics Ontology v0.1 (PROPOSED)

Status: DRAFT / NOT DEPLOYED. This document is an analytical ontology extension, not a prediction system or authorization source.

## Scope
Represent collective identity, social norms, symbolic constructs (including *egregore* as a metaphor), participation evidence, institutional change, and emergent coordination among agents. Durkheim's collective consciousness, social facts, collective effervescence, solidarity, and anomie are conceptual sources, not operationally validated numerical measures.

## Classes
- CollectiveBeliefSystem: claims and narratives attributed to groups with evidence and time boundaries.
- SocialNorm: documented expectations and institutional rules.
- CollectiveSymbolicConstruct: shared symbol, ritual, narrative, or egregore metaphor; never independent agency or authority.
- CollectiveActivationEvent: time-bounded observed gatherings or participation changes.
- SocialCohesion: explicitly defined network/coordination indicators, with sampling limitations.
- NormativeInstability: contested or weakening norms, supported by observations.
- SocialMovement: bounded group or coalition, never inferred from demographic identity alone.
- InstitutionalTransformation: observed changes in governance, law, leadership, or institutional practices.

## Evidence contract
Every claim records source URI or archival reference, observation time, collection method, geographic scope, population denominator where relevant, uncertainty, consent/legal basis, and verified/estimated/hypothetical status. Preserve contradictory sources. Never infer private beliefs, political affiliation, mental health, or individual threat propensity from group-level data. No identity-level persuasion profiles.

## Indicators (descriptive, not predictive)
Participation rate = independently verified active participants / defined eligible population. Avoid double counting, distinguish supporters from active participants, and record unknown denominators. Cohesion, semantic drift, narrative entropy, and institutional change require separately documented operational definitions. The 3.5% civil-resistance observation and 25% laboratory norm-shift findings are not universal thresholds and must not trigger automated decisions.

## Agentic emergent coordination
Represent interactions among agents, shared narratives, collective feedback loops, and institutional consequences without anthropomorphizing the group. Detect coordinated inauthentic behavior only with auditable evidence and appeal paths. Distinguish simulated, observed, and independently verified outcomes.

## Governance invariants
- Collective consensus != truth; popularity != legitimacy; mobilization != authorization.
- Egregore != identity, sovereign agent, mandate, or execution authority.
- Ontology and gBRAIN are descriptive/derived; WikiVault retains provenance; J-SPACE deliberation is non-authoritative.
- AEGIS/ATG control permission boundaries; 54-T verifies containment; VERITY reviews evidentiary claims; SENTINEL-6 examines feedback loops and drift.
- No covert political influence, fabricated grassroots activity, targeted manipulation, or autonomous consequential action.
- All execution requires identity -> mandate -> policy -> envelope -> receipt -> audit.
- No merge or production promotion without review, tests, and explicit human approval.

## Validation checklist
1. Schema tests for missing provenance, denominator, time, uncertainty, and status.
2. Contradictory-source preservation and unsupported-claim rejection.
3. Synthetic simulations separated from real-world evidence.
4. Cross-district federation and least-privilege read tests.
5. Negative tests proving collective symbolic constructs cannot gain authority.
6. Independent VERITY and 54-T review before integration.

## Integration targets (not claims of implementation)
WikiVault -> Ontology -> gBRAIN / J-SPACE; Thermodynamic System Ontology for drift/entropy; SENTINEL-6 for anomaly review; ATG/AEGIS/54-T for enforcement. This module does not override constitutional inheritance or district boundaries.
